Common Questions

What's included in the price?

Our prices include design, development, revisions, SSL certificate, and hosting setup. Additional services like custom photography or e-commerce functionality may have separate costs.

Can I upgrade later?

Absolutely. If you start with a Starter site and want to add more pages or features, we can expand your website. We'll discuss pricing based on what you add.

Do we offer payment plans?

Yes! We're flexible with payment. For projects over £400, we typically offer a 50% deposit upfront and 50% on completion. Let's discuss what works for you.

How long does a website take?

Starter sites typically take 1-2 weeks. Standard sites take 3-5 weeks. Premium projects vary based on complexity. We'll give you a clear timeline before we start.

What about ongoing costs?

We include hosting and SSL for the first 2-3 months, depending on your tier. After that, annual hosting costs are typically £60-£150 depending on your site. We handle renewals for you.

Do you offer maintenance or updates?

We offer a maintenance retainer starting at £50/month for updates, security patches, and technical support. Or you can contact us ad-hoc for specific work.

Are there free hosting options?

Yes! For simpler websites, we can use GitHub Pages or other free hosting platforms. You'd only pay for your domain (~£10-15/year). However, more complex sites with advanced features may require paid hosting (£50-150/year). We'll discuss what's best for your specific needs.

Can I customize my package?

Absolutely. The tiers above are guidelines, not rules. If you need a mix of features from different packages, or have specific requirements, we're happy to create a custom plan. Just get in touch and we'll work out pricing together.
