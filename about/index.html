Our Philosophy

We believe the best websites come from genuine collaboration and transparency. Too often, businesses end up with generic sites that don't reflect who they really are. We wanted to change that.

Our Story

No-Tea Studio was founded by Vasile-Renato and Imran, two Computer Science students who saw a gap in the web design market. We noticed businesses were being sold generic AI websites with zero transparency about how they were built or what they were actually getting.

We decided to do it differently. We write custom code from the ground up, tailored specifically to your business. We use AI strategically – for styling refinement, color palettes, bulk updates, or optimization – but you're always in control. Every line of code is reviewed and customized by us. We're not feeding prompts into ChatGPT and calling it a website. We're building something real, with your input at every step.

We're young, we're ambitious, and we're transparent about our process. You're not a transaction; you're a collaborator.

How We Work

  1. Discovery We chat with you about your vision, goals, and needs
  2. Development We build your site with custom code, keeping you updated and making changes as we go
  3. Refinement Your feedback shapes every iteration until it's exactly what you wanted
  4. Launch Once you're happy, we get it live and make sure everything runs smoothly

Our AI Use

We're transparent about how we use AI. It's a tool that makes us more efficient, not a replacement for understanding and craftsmanship:

  • CSS & Styling We use AI for color palette suggestions, responsive design refinement, and styling optimization
  • Bulk Updates Adding features across multiple pages? AI helps us implement changes consistently
  • Optimization We use AI to debug, optimize performance, and suggest improvements
  • Collaboration AI helps us work faster so we can spend more time on what matters: understanding your business

The important bit: we review everything. AI generates ideas; we make the decisions. You get a website built by real people who understand your needs.

Our Ideal Client

You're perfect for us if you're:

  • A small business owner or solopreneur (hairdresser, coach, consultant, tradesperson, creative)
  • Looking for a professional online presence without the corporate agency price tag
  • Open to collaboration and genuine feedback
  • Value transparency and honest communication
  • Bootstrapping or budget-conscious but won't compromise on quality

We're best suited for businesses that understand they're working with talented young developers who are hungry to prove themselves – not an established agency. You get exceptional value and genuine care; we get the opportunity to build our portfolio and reputation. It's a genuine win-win.
